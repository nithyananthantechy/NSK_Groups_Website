Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

NSK Groups (“we”, “us”) is a sole proprietorship run by Nithyananthan Nagarajan in Erode, Tamil Nadu, India. Our IT services division is NITECHSPARK and our hiring technology division is NiteHire. This policy explains how we handle personal information on this website and when you contact us.

2. What we collect

Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company name, and the message you write in our contact form; the details you enter when you book a free IT health check through our Google Calendar booking page; and the messages you send us on WhatsApp.

We do not ask for passwords or sensitive personal information through this website.

3. Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to book and carry out a free IT health check or a paid service, to send information you asked for, to keep business and tax records, to improve this website, and to follow the law. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

Only with providers we need to run our work: Google (Calendar booking, Maps, email), WhatsApp (messaging), FormSubmit (website form), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They handle data under their own terms. We may also share information when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries: up to 2 years unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Client records: for as long as the service needs and as long as law and tax rules require.

6. Keeping it safe

We limit access to the people who need it, use strong passwords and two-step verification, and use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to show you the personal data we hold, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. Email us at the address below and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Client systems and data

When we do IT work for a client, we access their systems only with their written permission and only as far as the work needs. We do not copy or share client data, and we do not ask for passwords for a free health check.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses. This website is not meant for people under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
