About NSK Groups

A technology company built in Erode, Tamil Nadu — engineering practical IT support and modern software for businesses.

ORIGIN & PURPOSE

Built in Erode. Built for businesses.

NSK Groups is a technology company based in Erode, Tamil Nadu. We started with a clear belief: modern, dependable technology infrastructure should be accessible, transparent, and resilient for every business.

Instead of fragmented external providers, we organize our capabilities into specialized business divisions under one unified group. Through NITECHSPARK, we deliver hands-on IT support, automated backups, system security, and custom automation. Through NiteHire, we develop intelligent hiring and recruitment platforms. Through NiteOrbit, we explore upcoming frontiers in ground-control technology.

We focus on long-term relationships where we take full ownership of your technical health so you can concentrate on running your operations.
